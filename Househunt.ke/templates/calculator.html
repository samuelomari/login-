Pro Tips for Kenyan House Hunters

Demand a Stamped Deposit Receipt

Always ensure your security deposit and water deposit are written clearly in the tenancy agreement and receipted so you can claim a full refund when moving out.

Inspect Past Token Bills

Verify that the previous tenant did not leave unpaid debt on the KPLC meter. Test entering KSh 100 on the meter keypad to confirm the balance.

Negotiate Moving Trucks

For intra-Nairobi moves (e.g. Roysambu to Ruaka or Kilimani to Rongai), booking a 1-tonne pickup directly from a local stage typically costs KSh 3,000–5,000 including offloaders.
